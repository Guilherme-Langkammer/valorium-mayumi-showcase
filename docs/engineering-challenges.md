# Engineering Challenges

## Product presentation

Confectionery products benefit from clear photography, categories and optional image galleries. The user experience should remain consistent as items are selected or quantities change.

## Promotional rules

Eligibility should be determined by explicitly configured products and conditions. The cart should not repeatedly re-evaluate unrelated products in a way that causes confusing visual changes.

## Scheduling and fulfillment

Scheduled orders require clear business cutoffs, available dates, pickup/delivery selection and reliable state transitions.

## Administration

Operational dashboards, inventory, permissions and order management should remain separated from public customer interfaces.

## Validation

Screenshots establish the presence of UI elements only. Backend behavior, security properties, payment processing and third-party integrations require direct testing in a controlled environment.
